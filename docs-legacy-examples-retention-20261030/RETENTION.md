# Private historical examples retention

This private namespace retains 21 original files previously discoverable through
the public platform asset branch. Their exact paths, byte counts and SHA-256
identities are in `retention-index.json`. The five bounded inert archive
inventories and original command receipt remain private. The current private
examples delivery and its original recovery proof remain unchanged.

Archives keep their original source and qualification identities. Retention does
not rerun or renew qualification. The creation-time index does not assert a
future authenticated remote readback. No public history has been rewritten;
removing a later branch-tip route cannot erase earlier commits or cloned bytes.

文件路径、长度与SHA-256见同目录索引；原有私有示例交付及恢复证明保持不变。

入口和归档，不代表旧提交或既有克隆中的数据已被消除。下载需要私有仓库权限。
